## Tags (space separated)

```
lithuanian lietuvių lietuviu a2 vocabulary audio tts kirtis pusiaukele beginner european-framework
```

Rationale: `lithuanian` and both spellings of `lietuvių` (AnkiWeb does not fold
diacritics, so a search for `lietuviu` will not match `lietuvių`); `a2` is the
level people actually search; `kirtis` is what a Lithuanian learner searches for
stress marks; `pusiaukele` names the textbook the word list follows.
